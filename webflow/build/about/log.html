Flight log

How a philosophy student ended up building interactive 3D for the web.

OriginWaypoint 01

A philosophy degree

I studied how to ask better questions, take arguments apart and put them back together. Turns out that’s most of design.

IgnitionWaypoint 02

Taught myself design

No design school. Just curiosity, a lot of late nights and pulling apart work I admired to see how it was made.

LiftoffWaypoint 03

Then taught myself code

When a design needed something the tools couldn’t do, I learned to build it. That’s still how I pick up every new skill.

OrbitWaypoint 04

Webflow, WebGL + motion

Now I plan, design in Figma and build in Webflow from the ground up: interactive 3D, CMS systems and interactions that help people find what they came for.
